Smart Obstacle Narrator

Real-time assistive obstacle detection that speaks.

  • Real-time vision system using Ultralytics YOLO11 and ByteTrack that detects and tracks obstacles and announces them through spoken alerts.
  • Monocular proximity (near, medium, far) and direction (left, ahead, right) estimation with a perspective walking corridor and hazard-prioritization scoring. Alerts are event-based with cooldowns and deduplication.
  • Streamlit and WebRTC web demo with browser text-to-speech and live FPS and latency metrics. Modular, YAML-configurable codebase with a pytest suite.

FrameCheck

Deepfake and AI-generated face detection.

  • Built and trained a 3-block CNN in PyTorch to classify real faces against StyleGAN-generated faces on the 140k Real and Fake Faces dataset.
  • Reached 90.85% accuracy and 90.85% F1 on a balanced 20k held-out test set, then analysed the performance drop on external images caused by distribution shift.
  • Deployed a Streamlit app where you upload an image and get a real or fake prediction with a confidence score.

  • Streamlit
